# Field Training — Sumter County EMS

The portal humans open. The spreadsheet stays the vault.

## What it is

**County badge first. Program name second: Field Training.**

| Role | Screen | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trainee | My chart | Where I am, what’s waiting on me |
| FTO | Tonight | Who I rode with → file for that person |
| Training Division | Waiting on you | Decisions · enroll · advance phase · clear for the truck |
| Supervisor | My shift | Who’s silent tonight |
| Medical Director | Clinical | Urgent concerns |

## Clear for the truck

**Released from training** means they finished every phase and the required skills and may ride as an **independent partner**. It is graduation — not leaving the job.

Status written: `Cleared / Independent`. Archive keeps who / when / why.
